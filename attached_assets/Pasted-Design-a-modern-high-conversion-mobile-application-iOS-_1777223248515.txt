Design a modern, high-conversion mobile application (iOS & Android) for a freelance marketplace specialized in e-commerce services.

🧠 Core Concept:

The app connects two types of users:

Service Providers (Freelancers) who sell predefined services with fixed pricing.
Clients (Business Owners / Store Owners) who browse, purchase, or request services.

The platform takes a commission from each transaction.

🎯 Main Features:
👤 Authentication:
Sign up / Login (Email, Google, Apple)
User selects role:
Freelancer (Service Provider)
Client (Buyer)
🧑‍💻 Freelancer Side:
Create profile (bio, skills, portfolio, ratings)
Add services:
Service title
Description
Price packages (Basic / Standard / Premium)
Delivery time
Add-ons
Dashboard:
Earnings
Active orders
Completed orders
Chat with clients
Notifications
Wallet (after commission deduction)
🛒 Client Side:
Browse services by categories:
Shopify / Salla store setup
Ads management (TikTok, Meta)
SEO optimization
Product photography
Branding
Advanced filters (price, rating, delivery time)
Service details page
Order & checkout
Request custom service
Chat with freelancers
Order tracking
💳 Payment System:
Secure checkout
Commission system (platform takes %)
Wallet system for freelancers
Payment methods (Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay)
🌍 Languages:
Fully bilingual:
Arabic (RTL layout)
English (LTR layout)
Smooth language switching
Proper font pairing:
Arabic: Cairo / Tajawal
English: Inter / Poppins
🎨 UI/UX Design Style:
Modern, clean, minimal, high-trust
Inspired by Fiverr + Upwork but more localized for e-commerce niche
🎨 Color Palette:
Primary: Deep Purple or Royal Blue (#5B3EFF or #2F6BFF)
Secondary: Soft gradient (Purple → Blue)
Accent: Neon Green or Orange for CTAs
Background: Light (White / Soft Gray)
Dark mode included
✨ Animations & Microinteractions:
Smooth page transitions
Button hover & tap animations
Loading skeletons
Success animation after purchase
Floating CTA buttons
Subtle motion in onboarding screens
📱 Key Screens:
Splash Screen

Home (Marketplace)

🚀 Extra Smart Features:
AI recommendation for services
“Top Rated” & “Trending” sections

🎯 Goal:

Design the app to maximize:

Trust
